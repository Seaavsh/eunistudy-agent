# Sources and scoring

Additional catalogue sources: the Italian Ministry of Universities and Research (MUR), [Offerta formativa 2010–2025](https://dati-ustat.mur.gov.it/dataset/bed0c71e-9f86-4a0f-a266-963b6f7bbbd2), is filtered to academic year 2025/26 and transformed to JSON. MUR's [open data documentation](https://ustat.mur.gov.it/media/1126/presentazionemiur_ferrara-20-novembre2017.pdf) identifies Italian Open Data License 2.0; this is a historical snapshot, not current admissions information. The Netherlands [DUO higher education register](https://data.overheid.nl/en/dataset/ho_opleidingsoverzicht) is filtered and deduplicated from the original and is licensed CC BY 4.0. German accreditation records come from EQAR DEQAR under PDDL; details and attribution are in `vendor/DEQAR-SOURCE.md`. The Hipo MIT copyright and license notice is retained in `vendor/HIPO-LICENSE.txt`.

The 24 university names, cities and official homepages in program cards are manually assembled sample identities. The full Hipo directory snapshot is retained in vendor and university records for the three destinations are imported into the local search index. Source: https://github.com/Hipo/university-domains-list (MIT license). Hipo does not verify program details. All 48 program titles, tuition, living costs, fees, intakes, GPA and IELTS thresholds are generated illustrative data and must not be treated as real offerings. No scholarship amounts are asserted.

Immigration references checked 2026-10-31: Germany https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/visa-recognition/visa-types/studying ; Italy https://www.universitaly.it/it/studenti-stranieri ; Canada https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/study-canada/study-permit/eligibility.html . Admission and visa eligibility are separate. Check primary sources for current guidance.

Rates: https://frankfurter.dev/ . The last successful EUR, CAD and USD rates are cached locally with their date. No rate is fabricated if the service cannot be reached.

Version 1 score, only when essential inputs are valid and sample requirements are met: 10 + 80 × (0.5 × GPA/scale + 0.3 × subject match + 0.2 × IELTS/9), rounded. Subject match is 1 for the same field or 0.35 otherwise. GPA scales supported: 4, 5, 20, 100. This is a demonstration heuristic, not a calibrated probability. It does not evaluate visa success or budget; affordability is computed separately. Foreign GPA conversion is a simplified ratio and not an official equivalence.
